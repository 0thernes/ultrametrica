## 2. Pre-Registered Hypotheses (`Experiment I`)

### Family A — Statistical Rejection of Random-Matrix GUE/GOE Thermalization in $H_\theta$ (`B28`–`B31`)

- **Treatment Arm:** Intra-sector adjacent level spacing ratios $r_j = \min(\delta_j, \delta_{j+1}) / \max(\delta_j, \delta_{j+1})$ inside the top-level valuation-residue sectors $W_{0,m}$ of $H_{\theta=0.5}$ at $\alpha \in \{1.0, 2.0\}$ for:
  - **H1:** $(p, n, m) = (5, 4, 2)$ ($\dim W_{0,2} = 125$, predicted picket-fence $r_0 = 1/4 = 0.25$) vs. matched-dimension $125 \times 125$ GUE random Hermitian control matrices.
  - **H2:** $(p, n, m) = (5, 4, 1)$ ($\dim W_{0,1} = 125$, predicted picket-fence $r_0 = 2/3 \approx 0.6667$) vs. matched-dimension $125 \times 125$ GOE random real symmetric control matrices.
  - **H3:** $(p, n, m) = (2, 7, 1)$ ($\dim W_{0,1} = 64$, predicted picket-fence $r_0 = 0$) vs. matched-dimension $64 \times 64$ GOE random real symmetric control matrices.
  - **H4 (Quadratic Dispersion $\alpha = 2.0$):** $(p, n, m) = (5, 4, 2)$ at $\alpha = 2.0$ vs. matched-dimension $125 \times 125$ GUE control matrices.
- **Control Arm & Fixed Seeds (`B28`):** $K = 20$ independent fixed-seed random Hermitian GUE / real symmetric GOE control matrices (`seeds = [1001..1020]`) generated by a deterministic Box-Muller seeded PRNG (`mulberry32`), diagonalized by the exact same Jacobi solver as the treatment arm.
- **Primary Metric:** Difference in sample mean spacing ratio $\Delta \langle r \rangle = \langle r \rangle_{\text{Adelic}} - \langle r \rangle_{\text{RMT}}$ per seed ($N_{\text{seeds}} = 20$ paired/per-seed contrasts against the deterministic treatment spectrum, plus two-sample Mann-Whitney U & 10,000-resample Monte Carlo permutation test on the full vector of individual spacing ratios $\{r_j\}$).
- **Statistical Test (`B29`):** Two-sided Monte Carlo permutation test (`10,000` seeded permutations, `seed = 20261125`) comparing the empirical treatment spacing ratios $\{r_j\}$ against the pooled 20-seed RMT control spacing ratios, plus exact sign/Wilcoxon check over the 20 per-seed control means.
- **Effect Size & 95% CI (`B30`):** Difference of means $\Delta \langle r \rangle$, Cliff's $\delta$, and Cohen's $d$, each paired with a seeded `10,000`-resample percentile bootstrap 95% confidence interval (`seed = 20261125`).
- **Multiple-Comparison Correction (`B31`):** Holm-Bonferroni step-down family-wise error rate adjustment across all 4 hypotheses (`alpha_FWER = 0.01`).
- **Decision / Falsification Rule:** A hypothesis is **confirmed** if and only if its Holm-Bonferroni adjusted $p_{\text{adj}} < 0.01$ AND the 95% bootstrap CI for $\Delta \langle r \rangle$ excludes $0$ in the predicted direction ($< 0$ for H1, H3, H4; $> 0$ for H2). Otherwise, the hypothesis is **refuted** and reported as a negative result.
- **Stopping Rule:** Fixed exact design (`K = 20` seeds, `10,000` permutations, `10,000` bootstrap resamples); zero sequential peeking or seed dropping.
