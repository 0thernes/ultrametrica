### 2. Why did you avoid Mathlib in the Lean 4 proofs?
Zero Mathlib yields three practical advantages for independent verification: (1) a clean build finishes in **~12 seconds** instead of downloading gigabytes of `.olean` caches; (2) every definition in [`lean/UltrametricaProofs/Spec.lean`](../lean/UltrametricaProofs/Spec.lean) is 100% transparent and readable in `205` lines without navigating deep typeclass hierarchies; and (3) `lake check --paranoid` and `lake comparator --paranoid` verify `Solution.lean` against `Challenge.lean` across **6 independent Lean kernels** (`Lean 4.35.0`, `leanchecker`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda_bin`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`) with zero external dependency bloat. See [`docs/adr/0001-zero-mathlib-lean4.md`](adr/0001-zero-mathlib-lean4.md).

### 3. How do I know the Lean 4 proofs aren't vacuous, bench-maxxed, using `sorry`, or proving watered-down statements?
Five independent defenses prevent proof gaming:
1. **Honest 4-Bucket Accounting (`202` Audited Declarations):** We never inflate declaration totals by double-counting comparator bindings or hiding helper lemmas. Our `202` audited declarations are reported explicitly as **`127` core theorems & corollaries** + **`56` zero-Mathlib helper lemmas** + **`7` adversarial false-variant refutations** (`AdversarialTests.lean`) + **`12` isolated Palomar comparator bindings** (`Challenge.lean` ↔ `Solution.lean`) across **8 proof modules**. Every single one of the 13 `.lean` files is `< 1,000` lines.
2. **Verbatim 5-second axiom check:** Run `cd lean && lake env lean VerifyAxioms.lean` — every single one of the **202** declarations uses only `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` (zero `sorryAx`, zero `Lean.ofReduceBool`/`native_decide`, zero custom axioms).
3. **Isolated 6-kernel comparator (`Challenge.lean` vs. `Solution.lean`):** `Challenge.lean` imports *only* `Spec.lean` (definitions only, zero proofs), directly specifies tight primal-dual $W_1$ existence (Targets 3 & 7), full arbitrary-dimension $L$ complex Hermitian Sherman-Morrison (Target 5), finite-graph edge-sum curved Gauss-Bonnet conservation (Target 11), actual discrete Vladimirov/Kozyrev-Haar eigenfunctions & `[D_K, J] = 0` (Target 12), and concrete kernel-checked non-vacuity witnesses across **all 10** theorem targets (`target_nonvacuity_witnesses`), matched identically against sorry-free `Solution.lean`.
4. **Live Negative Tamper-Rejection Gate (`scripts/test_lean_negative.py`):** CI mutates temporary copies of `Solution.lean` (injecting `sorry`) and `Challenge.lean` (weakening a target) to prove the pipeline actually rejects tampered proofs rather than passing vacuously.
5. **Line-by-line Statement Fidelity Audit:** [`docs/statement-fidelity.md`](statement-fidelity.md) maps every Lean theorem literally to the textbook/paper mathematical statement and states every hypothesis explicitly.
