## 1. Zero-Trust Architecture (`Spec.lean` → `Challenge.lean` vs. `Solution.lean`)

- **[`lean/UltrametricaProofs/Spec.lean`](../lean/UltrametricaProofs/Spec.lean):** Contains *only* pure mathematical definitions (`101` lines, zero theorems, zero tactics, zero `sorry`).
- **[`lean/Challenge.lean`](../lean/Challenge.lean):** Imports *only* `UltrametricaProofs.Spec` and states the 8 canonical verification targets with intentional `sorry` placeholders. It imports **none** of the 4 proof modules.
- **[`lean/Solution.lean`](../lean/Solution.lean):** Proves all 8 targets in `Ultrametrica.ComparatorTarget` with **zero `sorry`**, **zero `admit`**, **zero `native_decide`**, **zero custom axioms**, and `autoImplicit = false` / `relaxedAutoImplicit = false`.
- **[`lean/comparator.json`](../lean/comparator.json):** Enforces exact definitional equality between `Challenge` and `Solution` across all 8 theorem signatures with permitted axioms strictly limited to `["propext", "Quot.sound", "Classical.choice"]`.
