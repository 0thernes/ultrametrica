# Lean 4 Statement-Fidelity & Non-Vacuity Audit (`docs/statement-fidelity.md`)

> Independent AI Research Playbook (`v1.3`) Section 1 & Palomar Registry compliance. Every formal theorem in `lean/Challenge.lean` and `lean/UltrametricaProofs/` (`v1.5.0`, `149` audited declarations across Palomar `module` files) is audited line-by-line against its literal mathematical statement, checked for hidden vacuous hypotheses, paired with explicit concrete witnesses, and sharpened by 5 kernel-checked adversarial false-variant negations. Verdicts for Targets 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 were corrected after the 2026-11-26 statement audit, which found that several Lean statements are narrower than the paper theorems that cite them (see [`../ERRATA.md`](../ERRATA.md)).

## 1. Zero-Trust Architecture (`Spec.lean` → `Challenge.lean` vs. `Solution.lean`)

- **Palomar `module` System:** Every `.lean` file in `lean/` begins with `module` and uses explicit `public import` / `public theorem` / `@[expose] public def` declarations, with `autoImplicit = false` and `relaxedAutoImplicit = false`.
- **[`lean/UltrametricaProofs/Spec.lean`](../lean/UltrametricaProofs/Spec.lean):** Contains *only* pure mathematical definitions (zero theorems, zero tactics, zero `sorry`), including `finSum` and `genBridgeTransportCost` for multi-edge inhomogeneous Kantorovich duality.
- **[`lean/Challenge.lean`](../lean/Challenge.lean):** `< 150` lines (`< 10 KiB`), imports *only* `UltrametricaProofs.Spec` and states all **10 canonical verification targets** with intentional `sorry` placeholders. It imports **none** of the 5 proof modules.
- **[`lean/Solution.lean`](../lean/Solution.lean):** Proves all **10 targets** in `Ultrametrica.ComparatorTarget` with **zero `sorry`**, **zero `admit`**, **zero `native_decide`**, and **zero custom axioms**.
- **[`lean/comparator.json`](../lean/comparator.json):** Enforces exact definitional equality between `Challenge` and `Solution` across all 10 theorem signatures with permitted axioms strictly limited to `["propext", "Quot.sound", "Classical.choice"]`.
