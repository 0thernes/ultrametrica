## Verification Tiers

- **`kernel-checked`:** Machine-verified in pure Lean 4 (`v4.35.0`, Palomar `module`, zero Mathlib, no `sorry` outside the statement-only `Challenge.lean`, zero `native_decide`, `autoImplicit = false`, axioms within `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`) across `149` audited declarations (`139` core theorems across 5 modules incl. `AdversarialTests.lean` + `10` isolated `Spec`/`Challenge`/`Solution` targets). All are kernel-checked by Lean (`lake build`, `leanchecker`); `lake check --paranoid` also runs in Ubuntu CI, and the 10 comparator targets in `lean/comparator.json` (with their dependency closure) were also accepted by 6 independent kernels (`lean4`, `leanchecker`, `lean4lean`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`, `nanoda_bin`) via `lake comparator --paranoid` in CI run `45747436539`. The 149 count includes literal or definitional identities used as sanity witnesses (e.g. `even_prime_self_conjugate : (1 : Nat) = 2 - 1`), and 7 public helper lemmas are not listed by name in `VerifyAxioms.lean`. A Lean statement concerns the repository's own definitions; where a row also carries `exact-rational` or `numerical`, the row says which part is kernel-checked.
- **`exact-rational`:** Independently verified with **zero floating-point rounding error** in Python standard library arbitrary-precision `int`, `fractions.Fraction`, and $\mathbb{Q}(i)$ (`scripts/verify_exact.py`).
- **`pre-registered statistical`:** Pre-registered in git-sealed [`docs/preregistration.md`](docs/preregistration.md) (`c171b1b`) prior to execution, evaluated with 20-seed GUE/GOE controls, 10,000-resample permutation/bootstrap CIs, Cliff's $\delta$, Cohen's $d$, and Holm-Bonferroni correction (`scripts/stats_eval.mjs`).
- **`numerical`:** Verified in IEEE-754 double precision via `test/verify.mjs`, `test/mutants.mjs` (8/8 killed), `test/properties.mjs` (3,400+ cases incl. 200 curved-BTZ Dijkstra stress trials), `test/golden.mjs`, `reference/padic_oracle.py`, and zero-error strict `tsc --noEmit -p jsconfig.json`.
- **`prior work`:** Established literature explicitly credited in [`paper/refs.bib`](paper/refs.bib) (no priority claimed).
