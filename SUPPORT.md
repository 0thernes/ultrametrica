## Before Opening an Issue

1. Check [`docs/faq.md`](docs/faq.md) and [`docs/glossary.md`](docs/glossary.md) for common mathematical, physical, and setup questions.
2. Check [`REPLICATION.md`](REPLICATION.md) and [`docs/configuration.md`](docs/configuration.md) if reproducing results or launching the local server.
3. Check [`CLAIMS.md`](CLAIMS.md) and [`LIMITATIONS.md`](LIMITATIONS.md) for what is formally proved vs. numerical vs. explicitly not claimed.

## Where to Report

- **Bugs, errors, gaps, holes, or blindspots:** Open an issue using the [Bug or Finding template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug.yml) and select one of the 10 finding classes.
- **Mathematical counterexamples or theorem refutations:** Open an issue using the [Proof Challenge / Falsification template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/falsification.yml).
- **Research extensions or feature ideas:** Open an issue using the [Feature / Research Proposal template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/feature.yml).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** Follow [`.github/SECURITY.md`](.github/SECURITY.md) — do not post unpatched vulnerabilities publicly; email `0_0@0thernes.art`.
